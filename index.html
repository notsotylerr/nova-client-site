Questions

Is Nova Client safe to sign in with?

Yes. You sign in on Microsoft's official login page, so Nova never gets your password. Login tokens only stay on your own computer.

Do I need to own Minecraft?

Yes. You need a Microsoft account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition.

How do I remove Nova's access to my account?

Go to account.live.com/consent/Manage, find NOVA and remove it. You can sign in again at any time.

Can I use more than one account?

Yes. Add as many Microsoft accounts as you like and switch between them from the launcher. Each one signs in through Microsoft's official page.

Will Nova improve my FPS?

Nova includes performance optimisations designed to give smoother, higher frame rates. Results depend on your hardware and settings.
