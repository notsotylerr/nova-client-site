Questions

Is NOVA safe to sign in with?

Yes. You sign in on Microsoft's official login page, so NOVA never gets your password. Sessions stay on your own computer, in Windows Credential Manager.

Sign-in says "Invalid app registration". What does that mean?

Minecraft's services must approve each third-party launcher before it can sign players in. NOVA's approval request has been submitted and is waiting for review. Until it's approved, sign-in can fail with an HTTP 403 error.

Do I need to own Minecraft?

Yes. You need a Microsoft account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition.

How do I remove NOVA's access to my account?

Go to account.live.com/consent/Manage, find NOVA and remove it. You can sign in again at any time.

Can I use more than one account?

Yes. Add accounts from the Accounts page and your saved accounts stay available between launches. Each one signs in through Microsoft's official page.

Will NOVA improve my FPS?

NOVA includes performance tools designed to give smoother, higher frame rates. Results depend on your hardware and settings.

Where do the mods come from?

The Browse page lists releases from Modrinth. Mods, modpacks, textures, shaders and data packs are made by their own authors.
